Lumen Streets繁體中文

Put a night on your screen

Open Capture, choose the ratio and move the map to compose. PNG offers a 1920, 2560 or 3840-pixel longest edge. Video offers 1920 or 2560 pixels, 30 seconds to five minutes at 30 fps; the browser selects MP4/AVC or WebM/VP9. GIF is a six-second, 720-pixel preview at 10 fps.

Composition

Use a desktop, ultrawide, square, tablet, phone or custom ratio. Optional titles, landmark names and soft edge shading share the same framing in every format. Export completion or cancellation restores the live scene.

Apply the file

Use your system wallpaper picker for PNG. Video needs a compatible wallpaper player; supported formats and lock-screen behaviour depend on the device and player. Lumen Streets creates files and does not install a wallpaper app or change system settings. Live Photo conversion is not included.

Storage and sharing

Large videos need browser temporary-file storage. Try a shorter or smaller export if storage or encoding fails. Videos repeat with a cut. Keep the source credits included in the export and the license link alongside publicly shared work.

把夜景設為桌布

開啟匯出，選擇比例並移動地圖構圖。PNG 最長邊可選 1920、2560 或 3840 px；影片可選 1920 或 2560 px，30 秒至五分鐘、每秒 30 格，由瀏覽器選擇 MP4/AVC 或 WebM/VP9。GIF 為六秒、720 px、每秒 10 格的分享預覽。

構圖

可用桌面、超寬、正方形、平板、手機或自訂比例。標題、地標名稱與柔和邊緣壓暗共用相同構圖；完成或取消匯出後會還原即時場景。

套用檔案

PNG 可用系統桌布選擇器設定。影片需搭配相容的動態桌布播放器，格式及鎖定畫面支援依裝置與 App 而異。Lumen Streets 只產生檔案，不會安裝 App 或更改系統設定，也不提供 Live Photo 轉換。

儲存空間與分享

較大的影片需要瀏覽器暫存檔支援；空間或編碼不足時請縮短時間或降低解析度。影片並非無縫循環。公開分享時請保留匯出內的來源標示與授權連結。
