# v0.2.0 — Buildings and landmarks

Aerial Gold gains building depth and six recognizable landmarks, with a shared aerial camera for streets, structures and moving lights.

- Shaded facades, rooftop details, open tower frames and subtle shoreline reflections.
- Taipei 101, Sapporo TV Tower, Oriental Pearl Tower, Shanghai Tower, Shanghai World Financial Center and Jin Mao Tower.
- New scenes face north. Saved aerial-7 and aerial-8 scenes keep their view; **Scene → Update scene style** upgrades older artwork while preserving the camera and traffic.
- **Screen ratio** uses the full display dimensions for wallpaper exports, including portrait screens.
- Refreshed gallery and covers, with temporary packaging files cleaned up after each run.

Download `lumen-streets-v0.2.0-site.tar.gz` for self-hosting with Node.js 24, or `lumen-streets-v0.2.0-source.tar.gz` to build from source. Verify downloads with the accompanying `.sha256` files. See [Hosting](https://github.com/Gway0521/lumen-streets/blob/v0.2.0/docs/HOSTING.md) for setup.

Large scenes and video exports can use substantial memory and temporary storage. The updated renderer has been checked in desktop Edge, WebKit and on a physical Android device. Physical iPhone validation is pending. Video encoding depends on the browser, and clips repeat with a cut. See [Compatibility](https://github.com/Gway0521/lumen-streets/blob/v0.2.0/docs/COMPATIBILITY.md).
